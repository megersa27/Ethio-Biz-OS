# EthioBiz OS

EthioBiz OS is a smartphone-first business management system designed
to help Ethiopian small and medium-sized businesses manage their daily
business operations.

## Project Status

Current development phase:

**Phase 5 — Backend Development**

## Product Scope

The MVP focuses on:

- Authentication
- Dashboard
- Product management
- Inventory management
- Sales management
- Customer management
- Customer credit
- Expense management
- Business reports
- Settings
- Offline-capable operation

## Target Businesses

Initial target businesses:

- Mini-markets
- Supermarkets
- Pharmacies
